Terra Design System

Terra is the shared visual language for the field-operations product suite — a light, warm-neutral UI built around one accent (lime) and real field photography. Screens live on paper-white and soft grey-green grounds; ink is reserved for small high-contrast moments — a primary button, a circular action — and for text set over imagery. Every screen is composed exclusively from the tokens and components documented here, so design and implementation never drift apart.

Red plays two separate parts. --red-500 is a signal — the recording dot, a live badge — where nothing sits on top of it. --danger (--red-600) is the action colour, one step darker so white button text clears AA. Reaching for red-500 on a button is the mistake this split exists to prevent.

Buttons

Dark is the primary action color across the product; lime is reserved for the single highest-priority CTA on a screen.

<button class="btn btn-dark">Confirm & save log</button>
<button class="btn-icon btn-icon-lime">…</button>

Destructive

For actions that lose work. btn-danger is the irreversible confirm itself; btn-danger-soft is for a destructive action that is not the loudest thing on the screen. Neither carries a glow — a delete button should never look like the thing you want tapped.

<button class="btn btn-danger">Delete log</button>
<button class="btn btn-danger-soft">Discard recording</button>
Pair the destructive confirm with a quiet way out — btn-white or btn-ghost for Cancel — and never make the destructive button the only one in a dialog. --danger resolves to --red-600, not --red-500: white on red-500 is 3.9:1 and fails AA at 14.5px bold, while red-600 clears it at 5.1:1. --red-500 stays the signal colour for the recording dot and other indicators, where that rule does not apply.
